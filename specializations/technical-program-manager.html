Overview

What this role owns

Drive technically complex execution across engineering teams, architecture and systems.

This role typically coordinates business and functional stakeholders, aligns workstream owners, makes dependencies visible, and drives decisions that protect the intended outcome.

Core responsibilities

  • Engineering dependency maps
  • Technical trade-off decisions
  • Integration and release readiness

Depending on the company, responsibilities may also include financial tracking, release readiness, program reviews and organizational change.

How success is measured

ReliabilityExample KPI
PerformanceExample KPI
Milestone predictabilityExample KPI

Metrics vary by company, program lifecycle and business priorities.

Example program

Scenario: A platform launch depends on APIs owned by multiple engineering teams.

Questions a program manager would ask

  • What business outcome and milestone are at risk?
  • Which dependencies or constraints are on the critical path?
  • What options are available and how do they affect cost, timing and quality?
  • Who owns the decision and when is it needed?
